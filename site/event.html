Terrain: Copernicus DEM. Height is boosted by NISAR's measured line-of-sight ground motion, hugely exaggerated. Coloured fringes: one colour cycle = 10 cm of motion. NISAR only sees the ground before and after, so the slider shows how much the ground moved, not how fast. Drag to orbit, ctrl/⌘ + scroll (or pinch) to zoom.
