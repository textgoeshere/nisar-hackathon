Lab: two ways to draw the line

Trial pages for three events. Each draws the line the title promises on a flat map and lets you cut across it: switch to Cut, drag from one side to the other, and the page plots what the radar measured along your stroke. Nothing in the main site links here or depends on these pages.

NISAR data are provisional. Solid red = measured by radar; dashed = not measured by radar; blank = no reading.

Atlas

A paper street atlas: relief, streets, local place names, the line in red, labels written on the map.

Three inks

A three-ink poster: the radar measurement printed as dots sized by how much changed, bare paper where there is no reading, one big number.
