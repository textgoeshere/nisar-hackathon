## Primary source and verified facts

- [GSJ/AIST Emergency field report No. 5 (第5報 緊急現地調査報告)](https://www.gsj.jp/hazards/earthquake/kumamoto2026/kumamoto2026-02.html), **photo 10**. The report opened 7 August 2026 and was updated 10 August 2026.
- [Original image](https://www.gsj.jp/hazards/earthquake/kumamoto2026/images/kumamoto2026-17.png). This is the photo-10 image linked by the report, despite the filename ending in `17`.
- Report authors, exactly as named: **赤井 東・太田耕輔・丸山 正・吉見雅行・吾妻 崇・二宮 啓** (Akai, Ota, Maruyama, Yoshimi, Azuma, Ninomiya), Geological Survey of Japan, AIST. The page credits the report collectively; it does not identify a separate individual photographer for photo 10.
- The report introduction explicitly dates the earthquake to **28 July 2026**.
- Photo 10 explicitly identifies the schoolyard of **Yatsushiro City Ryuho Elementary School (八代市立龍峯小学校), Okamachi, Yatsushiro, Kumamoto**.
- The caption states **94 cm of right-lateral offset**, using the schoolyard's painted white line as the reference. It also describes the associated mole track (a low ridge of disturbed ground).
- The caption explicitly states the photograph was taken **facing north on 31 July 2026**.
- The report describes a 30 July–3 August emergency survey and simple displacement measurements at selected locations using a tape measure and compass. It supplies no measurement uncertainty for this particular 94 cm value.

The relevant original caption is:

> 写真10 熊本県八代市岡町の八代市立龍峯小学校の校庭に見られる右横ずれ変位とずれによって生じたモールトラック。校庭に引かれていた白線を基準にした時の右横ずれ変位量は94cm。写真は北方向に向かって撮影。（2026年7月31日撮影）

- Field offset and satellite line-of-sight motion are different quantities at different spatial scales. Do not infer building damage, safety, or casualties from the photograph or radar.
