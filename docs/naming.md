# Naming the project

Decision brief, 15 November 2026. Candidates only: no repository rename or visibility change.

**Brief:** name a project connecting satellite-observed ground change with sourced ground accounts, with equal invitations to explore and create.

## 1. Reframe

- Name the human benefit, not the hackathon folder or data format.
- Name a bridge between scales: orbit to an identifiable place.
- Name a way to investigate, not a guarantee to explain every impact.
- Leave room for floods, fires, subsidence and eruptions, not just faults.

Drop three assumptions: NISAR need not appear in the product name (it belongs in the subtitle and credits); the team pun need not be the repo slug; visual drama is not scientific certainty.

## 3. Disrupt

- **What are we missing?** People may come for a place rather than a disaster: *Place in View*.
- **What would an outsider suggest?** A journalist might call it *Ground Dispatch*, not a radar viewer.
- **What if the opposite were true?** Lead from the schoolyard outward: *Places, Not Pixels*.
- **Remove the biggest constraint:** without hackathon or sensor identity, *Earth in Context* can grow beyond one dataset.
- **Absurd but useful:** *The Quiet Satellite* suits a silent film and reminds us measurements do not explain themselves; too oblique for the primary name.

## 4. Recombine

- **Groundstory** = storytelling + a mapped ground location.
- **Orbit to Ground** = the scale journey + the human destination.
- **Orbit & After** = satellite observation + before/after photography and consequences.
- **Earth, Annotated** = a map + editorial sourcing.

## 5. Evolve and shortlist

1. **Orbit to Ground** — recommended for immediate clarity. Repo: `orbit-to-ground`. Direct variation: *Orbit → Ground* in graphics; editorial variation: *Dispatches from Orbit*. Subtitle: **“Satellite change. Human context.”** Covers every event type without claiming to prove causes or to be an official NASA service.
2. **Groundstory** — shorter, warmer, more brand-like. Repo: `groundstory`. Variations: *Ground Stories* (collection), *Groundstory Lab* (creation tool). Subtitle: **“Earth change, connected to everyday places.”**
3. **Orbit & After** — most distinctive editorial tone. Repo: `orbit-and-after`. Variations: *Orbit / After* (visual identity), *The After Atlas* (collection-led). Subtitle: **“What changed here?”** Less immediately descriptive, so keep the subtitle.

Avoid *Ground Truth* (overclaims what the interpretation establishes), *Faultlines* (earthquake-specific), and *NISAR Explained* (sounds broader or more official than the project).

**Availability:** not trademark/domain cleared. General web search was unavailable during this pass. A GitHub repository search found two existing public uses of `groundstory`; that does not prevent use in another owner namespace but means it is not globally unique. Check the preferred name before treating it as a lasting brand. No availability claim has been made for the other candidates.
